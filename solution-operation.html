OPERATION

Voyage Report

Voyage Reports are used to manage shipping operations and calculate and maintain financial information during voyages. This feature helps the operations department to get daily reports for specific trips and keep track of the expenses involved. This feature calculates all costs real-time.

  • Real-time cost calculation during voyages
  • Daily reports for specific trips
  • Track all operational expenses
  • Maintain financial information during voyages
OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE

Time Efficiency & Complete Records

The biggest responsibility of any shipping trip is the operational cover. Voyage report helps in the role of efficient use of time in real-time reporting that is obtained directly from the operating fleet.

Voyage report records all activities that occur in a journey based on real time reports on electronic notebooks. Every event, expense, and operational decision is documented for complete audit trail.

  • Real-time reporting directly from operating fleet
  • Efficient time management for operational coverage
  • Complete activity recording on electronic notebooks
