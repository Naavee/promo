HAF Operation

HAF Operations provides quick and easy access to top level management, technical, commercial and procurement managers for every activity and requirement of your cruise journey.

Why Voyage Report?

Voyage Reports are used to manage shipping operations and calculate and maintain financial information during voyages. This feature helps the operations department to get daily reports for specific trips and keep track of the expenses involved. This feature calculates all costs real-time.

TIME EFFICIENCY

The biggest responsibility of any shipping trip is the operational cover. Voyage report helps in the role of efficient use of time in real-time reporting that is obtained directly from the operating fleet.

RECORD EVERYTHING

Voyage report records all activities that occur in a journey based on real time reports on electronic notebooks.

API INTEGRATION

These supporting features shape operational analysis data from multiple perspectives and sources. HAF Operation provides a path for shipping companies to connect data recorded on each tracking device such as location tracking, fuel control, RPM monitoring and other tracking support tools owned by your ship in shipping operations.
