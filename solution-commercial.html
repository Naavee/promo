HAF Commercial

HAF provides Pre-Fixture Analysis as a tool to analyze the feasibility of a shipment from the commercial side that you want to propose.The commercial function of the company will get sophisticated support with an estimate of the profit and loss that will occur from a single shipment.

This calculation analysis will cover every expense and income that will be involved from a trip. Inspection of shipping costs, bunker trends and simulation of operational expenses that will occur such as fuel or diesel oil expected on a trip.

WHY PRE-FIXTURE ?

SAVE TIME

Submission, review and approval of a trip can be arranged according to the person in charge to a certain level. Automatic email alerts notify users of updates to a trip. This simplifies the travel approval process, as less human communication is required in the approval process.

FIND THE OPTIMAL TRIP

HAF Enterprise uses advanced algorithms to find the optimal journey. Having a Pre-Fixture gives you a competitive edge when it comes to analyzing and repairing cargo. The trip comparison module creates an optimal cost-benefit analysis, facilitating comparisons between different trip simulations

>

CREATE DECISIONS FAST AND REASONABLE

Real-time applied communication supports submission, review and approval processes with Pre-Fixture analysis. Digital travel contract support forms the fast action of a project.
