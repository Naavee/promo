HAF FINANCE & ACCOUNTING
ALL IN ONE SOLUTION

An integrated reporting solution starting from the commercial, operational and procurement modules provides convenience in making payments and recording according to requests from each module. Automatic journaling provides a fast solution in an accounting bookkeeping according to shipping COA standards.

SPECIALIZATION FOR THE SHIPPING SECTOR

Developed for the Indonesian shipping industry, HAF Financials & Accounting is an advanced Financial Reporting system that handles multi-object, multi-company and multi-supplier transactions. The integration that is connected to the digital contract on the Pre-Fixture will minimize errors in inputting and adjusting taxes in accordance with the Regulation of the Directorate General of Taxes of the Republic of Indonesia. Systems that use a web-based environment; therefore multiple users can be supported in any location.
