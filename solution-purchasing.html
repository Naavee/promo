DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

Fully Electronic Purchasing Workflow

The software includes an Alerts feature, which enables a fully electronic purchasing workflow. From requisition to approval to delivery tracking, every step is automated and digitally recorded.

  • Automated requisition and approval process
  • Real-time purchase order tracking
  • Digital document management for all procurement
  • Integrated with vessel inventory systems
SEAMLESS INTEGRATION

Direct Integration with Every Vessel

The procurement system is directly integrated with every ship in your shipping operation. Each vessel can create requisitions, track orders, and manage inventory from their onboard systems.

  • Per-vessel procurement management
  • Centralized supplier database across fleet
  • Real-time inventory updates from vessels
  • Budget monitoring per vessel and fleet-wide
