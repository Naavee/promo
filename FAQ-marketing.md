# Bahasa Indonesia

## FAQ Umum

**Apa itu HAF ERP?**

HAF ERP adalah sistem terintegrasi yang dirancang khusus untuk perusahaan shipping dan logistik. Seluruh aktivitas operasional dapat dikelola dari satu platform, mulai dari komersial, operasional kapal, pengadaan, finance, sampai akuntansi.

**Untuk industri apa HAF ERP dirancang?**

HAF ERP fokus pada industri shipping line dan logistik, terutama yang mengoperasikan armada tug dan barge serta membutuhkan pengelolaan terpadu dari voyage sampai administrasi crew.

**Modul apa saja yang tersedia?**

Tersedia 8 modul: HAF Commercial, HAF Operation, HAF Fuel Control, HAF Purchasing, HAF Technical, HAF Finance, HAF Accounting, dan HAF Crewing. Semuanya saling terintegrasi.

**Apakah modul-modulnya saling terintegrasi?**

Iya. Data Commercial dan Operation otomatis mengalir ke Finance, jurnal dari Finance dan Purchasing masuk ke Accounting, sementara Fuel Control terhubung ke Purchasing untuk pencatatan bunker.

**Apakah ada biaya tambahan per modul?**

Tidak. Seluruh modul sudah termasuk dalam paket HAF ERP.

**Apakah implementasinya mudah?**

HAF ERP dirancang untuk siap pakai dengan setup dan pelatihan terstruktur, sehingga tim operasional dapat langsung produktif.

**Apakah user-friendly untuk staff non-IT?**

Iya. Tampilan dibuat sederhana dengan alur yang familiar untuk staff operasional di industri shipping.

**Cocok untuk perusahaan ukuran apa?**

Cocok untuk perusahaan shipping skala menengah hingga besar dengan armada multi-vessel dan operasional multi-divisi.

## FAQ per Modul

### HAF Commercial

**Apa itu HAF Commercial?**

Modul untuk mengelola siklus shipment, mulai dari pengajuan, persetujuan, analisa kelayakan, realisasi, sampai perbandingan antar shipment.

**Bagaimana modul ini membantu tim commercial?**

Memberi visibilitas penuh terhadap setiap shipment dalam satu platform, sehingga keputusan komersial dapat dibuat lebih cepat dan berbasis data.

**Apakah mendukung Freight Charter dan Time Charter?**

Iya. Realisasi shipment mendukung tipe Freight Charter dan Time Charter dalam satu modul.

**Apakah terintegrasi dengan Operation dan Finance?**

Iya. Data shipment otomatis terhubung ke Operation untuk voyage dan ke Finance untuk penerbitan invoice.

**Bisa untuk analisa kelayakan shipment?**

Bisa. Tersedia Analyze Shipment dan Business Analysis Dashboard, dilengkapi kalkulator cepat shipment.

**Apakah SPAL dapat dicetak otomatis?**

Iya. Dokumen Surat Perjanjian Angkutan Laut (SPAL) dapat dicetak langsung dari sistem.

---

### HAF Operation

**Apa itu HAF Operation?**

Modul untuk mencatat aktivitas operasional kapal selama voyage berlangsung, mulai dari penugasan tug dan barge sampai konsumsi bahan bakar.

**Bagaimana modul ini mendukung operasional harian?**

Tim operations dapat mencatat posisi kapal, kejadian harian, dan performa voyage secara terpusat, sehingga monitoring armada lebih akurat.

**Apakah laytime dan downtime dihitung otomatis?**

Iya. Sistem menyediakan perhitungan laytime dan downtime per voyage beserta laporannya.

**Apakah konsumsi bahan bakar dipantau?**

Iya. Konsumsi bahan bakar dan fresh water tercatat per voyage, lengkap dengan ringkasan total dan Remaining on Board (ROB).

**Apakah terintegrasi dengan modul lain?**

Iya. Data voyage terhubung ke Commercial untuk realisasi shipment dan ke Fuel Control untuk pencatatan bahan bakar.

**Cocok untuk armada tug dan barge?**

Iya. Modul ini secara khusus dirancang untuk operasional armada tug, barge, dan shipment.

---

### HAF Fuel Control

**Apa itu HAF Fuel Control?**

Modul yang menjaga akuntabilitas bahan bakar mulai dari permintaan crew, pembelian, sampai transfer antar lokasi.

**Bagaimana modul ini meningkatkan akuntabilitas bahan bakar?**

Setiap permintaan, pembelian, dan transfer fuel tercatat dalam satu alur, sehingga jejak konsumsi dan posisi stok bahan bakar transparan.

**Apakah mendukung transfer antar lokasi?**

Iya. Transfer dapat dilakukan antar office, warehouse, tug, dan barge.

**Apakah terhubung dengan Purchasing?**

Iya. Pencatatan penerimaan bunker terhubung langsung ke Purchase Order, dan jurnal AP otomatis mengalir ke Accounting.

**Apakah PPN dan PPH untuk PO bahan bakar dihitung otomatis?**

Iya. Sistem menghitung PPN dan PPH sesuai pengaturan termin pembayaran dalam Fuel Order.

---

### HAF Purchasing

**Apa itu HAF Purchasing?**

Modul untuk mengelola pengadaan barang dan jasa secara elektronik, mulai dari permintaan sampai pembayaran ke vendor.

**Bagaimana modul ini menyederhanakan pengadaan?**

Seluruh proses pengadaan terpusat dalam satu alur, sehingga tim procurement dapat memantau status setiap permintaan dengan cepat.

**Apakah mendukung approval bertingkat?**

Iya. Setiap dokumen pengadaan memiliki alur persetujuan berjenjang sesuai struktur organisasi.

**Apakah terintegrasi dengan Accounting?**

Iya. Jurnal AP otomatis mengalir ke Accounting setelah pengadaan disetujui dan dibayarkan.

**Cocok untuk pengadaan barang dan jasa sekaligus?**

Iya. Purchase Request dan Purchase Order untuk barang, sementara Working Request dan Working Order untuk jasa.

---

### HAF Technical

**Apa itu HAF Technical?**

Pusat pengelolaan kebutuhan teknis kapal dan office, mulai dari material, jasa, sampai pengajuan pembayaran lintas divisi.

**Apa beda HAF Technical dan HAF Purchasing?**

Purchasing fokus pada pengadaan barang untuk warehouse dan office. Technical fokus pada kebutuhan teknis kapal seperti maintenance dan jasa, lengkap dengan Service Request dan Service Order.

**Bagaimana modul ini mendukung maintenance kapal?**

Setiap kebutuhan teknis kapal terdokumentasi dalam alur formal, mulai dari permintaan, persetujuan, sampai pembayaran ke vendor.

**Apakah mendukung Service Request dan Service Order?**

Iya. Keduanya tersedia khusus di Technical untuk kebutuhan repair, maintenance, dan docking.

**Apakah Payment Request terintegrasi dengan Finance?**

Iya. Payment Request yang disetujui di Technical diteruskan ke Finance untuk diproses pembayarannya.

---

### HAF Finance

**Apa itu HAF Finance?**

Modul untuk mengelola transaksi finansial operasional perusahaan, mulai dari kontrak charter, invoice, sampai jurnal otomatis ke Accounting.

**Apakah mendukung invoice multi-tipe?**

Iya. Invoice untuk shipment, time charter, dan non-shipment tersedia dalam satu modul.

**Apakah otomatis terhubung dengan Accounting?**

Iya. Setiap transaksi finance yang disetujui akan menghasilkan jurnal otomatis di Accounting.

**Apakah mendukung credit note dan demurrage?**

Iya. Credit note dan demurrage dapat diterbitkan langsung dari modul Finance.

**Cocok untuk perusahaan shipping berbasis charter?**

Sangat cocok. SPAL, time charter invoicing, dan agency invoice sudah didukung sejak awal.

---

### HAF Accounting

**Apa itu HAF Accounting?**

Modul akuntansi terintegrasi yang menerima jurnal otomatis dari modul lain dan menyediakan laporan keuangan perusahaan.

**Apakah mendukung double-entry accounting?**

Iya. Modul ini mengelola Chart of Account, Ledger, dan jurnal berbasis double-entry standar.

**Apakah jurnal dari modul lain otomatis masuk?**

Iya. Jurnal otomatis tersedia dari Sales Invoice, Purchasing, TC Invoice, dan Other Transaction.

**Apakah laporan keuangan standar tersedia?**

Iya. Termasuk Trial Balance, General Ledger, Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, Cash Flow, beserta laporan aging AR dan AP.

**Apakah mendukung fixed asset?**

Iya. Tersedia register Fixed Asset beserta master Fixed Asset Category.

**Apakah ada penguncian periode fiskal?**

Iya. Date Lock Entries menjaga integritas data dengan mencegah input jurnal mundur.

---

### HAF Crewing

**Apa itu HAF Crewing?**

Modul untuk mengelola master data crew, sertifikat dengan masa berlakunya, dan penempatan crew ke kapal.

**Apa saja yang bisa dikelola tentang crew?**

Mulai dari biodata, role, bank, BPJS, ijazah, sertifikat, sampai penempatan ke tug beserta tanggal sign-on dan sign-off-nya.

**Apakah mendukung sertifikat crew dengan masa berlaku?**

Iya. Setiap crew dapat memiliki banyak sertifikat lengkap dengan tanggal masa berlakunya.

**Apakah mendukung perhitungan payroll crew?**

Modul ini fokus pada master data dan formula bonus crew. Perhitungan payroll otomatis tidak termasuk dalam scope modul.

**Apakah penempatan crew ke kapal tercatat?**

Iya. Setiap crew dapat ditempatkan ke tug beserta tanggal kontrak sign-on dan sign-off.

# English

## General FAQ

**What is HAF ERP?**

HAF ERP is an integrated system purpose-built for shipping and logistics companies. The entire operation can be managed from a single platform, covering commercial, vessel operations, procurement, finance, and accounting.

**Which industries is HAF ERP designed for?**

HAF ERP focuses on shipping lines and logistics providers, particularly fleet operators running tug and barge operations that need integrated management from voyage to crew administration.

**What modules are available?**

Eight modules: HAF Commercial, HAF Operation, HAF Fuel Control, HAF Purchasing, HAF Technical, HAF Finance, HAF Accounting, and HAF Crewing. All modules are integrated.

**Are the modules integrated with each other?**

Yes. Commercial and Operation data flows into Finance, Finance and Purchasing post automatic journals to Accounting, while Fuel Control connects to Purchasing for bunker recording.

**Is there an additional cost per module?**

No. All modules are included in the HAF ERP package.

**Is implementation easy?**

HAF ERP is designed for quick deployment with structured setup and training, so operational teams can become productive right away.

**Is it user-friendly for non-IT staff?**

Yes. The interface is straightforward and the workflows feel familiar to shipping operations teams.

**What company size is it suitable for?**

It fits mid-sized to large shipping companies with multi-vessel fleets and multi-division operations.

## Module FAQs

### HAF Commercial

**What is HAF Commercial?**

A module for managing the full shipment lifecycle, from request and approval to feasibility analysis, realization, and side-by-side comparison.

**How does this module help the commercial team?**

It provides full visibility of every shipment on one platform, so commercial decisions become faster and data-driven.

**Does it support Freight Charter and Time Charter?**

Yes. Shipment realization supports both Freight Charter and Time Charter within a single module.

**Is it integrated with Operation and Finance?**

Yes. Shipment data automatically flows to Operation for voyage recording and to Finance for invoice issuance.

**Can it analyze shipment feasibility?**

Yes. Analyze Shipment and the Business Analysis Dashboard are available, along with a quick shipment calculator.

**Can SPAL documents be printed automatically?**

Yes. The Sea Transport Agreement (SPAL) document can be printed directly from the system.

---

### HAF Operation

**What is HAF Operation?**

A module for recording all vessel activity during a voyage, from tug and barge assignment to fuel consumption tracking.

**How does this module support daily operations?**

The operations team can record vessel positions, daily events, and voyage performance centrally, giving more accurate fleet monitoring.

**Are laytime and downtime calculated automatically?**

Yes. The system provides per-voyage laytime and downtime calculation with corresponding reports.

**Is fuel consumption monitored?**

Yes. Fuel and fresh water consumption are tracked per voyage, complete with totals and a Remaining on Board (ROB) overview.

**Is it integrated with other modules?**

Yes. Voyage data connects to Commercial for shipment realization and to Fuel Control for fuel recording.

**Is it suitable for tug and barge fleets?**

Yes. The module is purpose-built for tug, barge, and shipment operations.

---

### HAF Fuel Control

**What is HAF Fuel Control?**

A module that safeguards fuel accountability from crew request and purchase to inter-location transfer.

**How does this module improve fuel accountability?**

Every fuel request, purchase, and transfer is captured in a single flow, giving clear visibility of consumption and stock position.

**Does it support transfers between locations?**

Yes. Transfers can be done between office, warehouse, tug, and barge.

**Is it connected to Purchasing?**

Yes. Bunker receipts are linked directly to Purchase Orders, and AP journals flow automatically into Accounting.

**Are VAT and withholding tax for fuel POs calculated automatically?**

Yes. The system calculates VAT and withholding tax in line with the payment terms set in the Fuel Order.

---

### HAF Purchasing

**What is HAF Purchasing?**

A module for managing electronic procurement of goods and services, from request to vendor payment.

**How does this module simplify procurement?**

The entire procurement process runs in one centralized flow, so the procurement team can track request status quickly.

**Does it support tiered approval?**

Yes. Every procurement document follows a tiered approval workflow that matches the organization structure.

**Is it integrated with Accounting?**

Yes. AP journals automatically flow into Accounting once procurement is approved and paid.

**Is it suitable for both goods and services procurement?**

Yes. Purchase Request and Purchase Order cover goods, while Working Request and Working Order cover services.

---

### HAF Technical

**What is HAF Technical?**

The central hub for vessel and office technical needs, covering materials, services, and cross-division payment requests.

**How is HAF Technical different from HAF Purchasing?**

Purchasing focuses on goods procurement for warehouse and office. Technical focuses on vessel technical needs such as maintenance and services, complete with Service Request and Service Order.

**How does this module support vessel maintenance?**

Every technical need is documented in a formal flow, from request and approval to vendor payment.

**Does it support Service Request and Service Order?**

Yes. Both are exclusively available in Technical for repair, maintenance, and docking services.

**Is Payment Request integrated with Finance?**

Yes. Approved Payment Requests in Technical are forwarded to Finance for payment processing.

---

### HAF Finance

**What is HAF Finance?**

A module for managing operational financial transactions, from charter contracts and invoices to automatic journals into Accounting.

**Does it support multi-type invoices?**

Yes. Invoices for shipment, time charter, and non-shipment are all covered in a single module.

**Is it automatically connected to Accounting?**

Yes. Every approved finance transaction generates an automatic journal in Accounting.

**Does it support credit notes and demurrage?**

Yes. Credit notes and demurrage can be issued directly from the Finance module.

**Is it suitable for charter-based shipping companies?**

Very much so. SPAL contracts, time charter invoicing, and agency invoicing are supported out of the box.

---

### HAF Accounting

**What is HAF Accounting?**

An integrated accounting module that receives automatic journals from other modules and produces the company's financial reports.

**Does it support double-entry accounting?**

Yes. The module manages Chart of Account, Ledger, and journals based on standard double-entry principles.

**Are journals from other modules posted automatically?**

Yes. Automatic journals are available from Sales Invoice, Purchasing, TC Invoice, and Other Transaction.

**Are standard financial reports available?**

Yes. Including Trial Balance, General Ledger, Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss, Cash Flow, along with AR and AP aging reports.

**Does it support fixed assets?**

Yes. A Fixed Asset register together with a Fixed Asset Category master is provided.

**Is there fiscal period locking?**

Yes. Date Lock Entries safeguards data integrity by preventing backdated journal entries.

---

### HAF Crewing

**What is HAF Crewing?**

A module to manage crew master data, certificates with validity dates, and crew placement on vessels.

**What aspects of the crew can be managed?**

Everything from biodata, role, bank, BPJS, ijazah, certificates, to tug placement together with sign-on and sign-off dates.

**Does it support crew certificates with validity dates?**

Yes. Each crew member can hold multiple certificates with their respective validity dates.

**Does it support crew payroll calculation?**

The module focuses on master data and bonus formulas for crew. Automated payroll calculation is outside the module's current scope.

**Is crew placement on vessels recorded?**

Yes. Each crew member can be assigned to a tug together with sign-on and sign-off contract dates.
